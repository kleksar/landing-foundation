# Editorial basis

Sources checked on 6 September 2026. These are references for maintaining the skill, not a required
research step for each client. The skill's workflow and Russian copy choices are our adaptation;
the sources do not validate this template or promise commercial results.

- [GOV.UK: Understand content design](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/understand-content-design/).
  The official guidance starts from what people need to find out or accomplish, then selects the
  amount and format of content. We use that decision criterion instead of a fixed section inventory.
  A government publishing guide does not determine the voice or sales strategy of a client brand.
- [NN/g: How People Read Online: New and Old Findings](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-people-read-online/),
  5 April 2020. The authors report their own series of eyetracking studies and describe persistent
  scanning behavior alongside changes in layout-dependent reading patterns. This supports meaningful
  headings and placing relevant information where it can be found. It does not prescribe one page
  length or layout, and it is not a conversion test of our Russian landing pages.
- [Mailchimp: Voice and Tone](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/).
  This brand's own guide distinguishes a consistent voice from tone adapted to the reader's situation
  and prioritizes clarity over forced entertainment. We apply the distinction, not Mailchimp's
  personality. Each client can have its own vocabulary, rhythm and degree of formality.

## Local decisions

Keep copy and its acceptance in `SITE_SPEC.md`; preserve concise project status in `PROJECT.md`.
Separate publishable text from missing inputs and provenance notes. These are repository choices,
motivated by handoff and the risk of displaying internal draft instructions on a client page.

Assess content semantically against a brief. A valid skill file, a word blacklist, a readability
number or an agent's taste does not prove useful or persuasive writing. Concrete unsupported claims,
misleading actions, missing decision information and unnecessary repetition are actionable findings.
Different content structure needs a reason in the task; changing headings alone is no evidence of
meaningful variation. Measure user understanding and business outcomes in later real use.
